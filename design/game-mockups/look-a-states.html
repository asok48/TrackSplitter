1 · Normal play

Top: only the score. Background: a deep violet glow while it's going steadily. Long notes have a slim tail; a slide shows a thin line between the two notes, a hammer-on an "h". Beat lines are faint, bar lines a little brighter. The string you're playing gets a white outline with its fret.

2 · Hot streak (×4)

The background brightens to blue and violet. The edges and strike line turn electric blue, a soft blue wash lifts off the board, the multiplier bar is full. Hits flash the button and throw a few small sparks.

3 · A miss

The missed note turns grey and keeps going; the button flashes red with a small "Miss · played B" label. The edges dim to amber, and the background fades to a dim, cold grey with a faint red tinge while it's going badly; it warms back up as you recover.

4 · Guitar chord

Chord notes are joined by a thin bar with the chord name above, in the same flat style.

5 · Pause and results

Same type and colours: plain panels, one accent button.
