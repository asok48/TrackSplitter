Now

The capsule in the game today.

1 · Waveform

The song's loudness etched into the glass, mirrored like an audio waveform: faint ahead, so you see the loud parts and breaks coming; lit in how each bar went behind you. The big moments are small bright flares on the frame.

2 · Alive

It plays along: the fill breathes on the kicks, its front takes your multiplier's colour (grey, yellow, orange, blue aurora at x4), every hit throws a spark off the knob, a miss glitches it red, and every 10 in a row a pulse of light runs back along the fill.

3 · Warp

You fly it: stars stream past inside the glass ahead (faster as the song gets louder), the knob is an energy core with a light trail, and the big moments are gates on the track that flare as you pass through them.

4 · All three

Waveform + Alive + Warp together.

5 · All three, polished

The same, cleaner: the waveform a smooth shape with a bright edge, x4 a blue-violet shimmer inside it (no ribbons over it), fewer and softer stars behind it, the gates as rings of light that burst open as you pass, a soft glow round the frame in your multiplier's colour, sparks as light streaks, an inner shadow for depth.
