A · Skyline

Each bar a column as tall as its notes, so you see the busy parts and the breaks coming. Played columns light up in how they went, the Encore phrases ahead glow gold, a playhead with the time on it.

B · Glass capsule

A framed glass tube that fills as you play, in how each bar went, with a sheen running through it. Notches where the song's parts change, gold gems on the frame for Encore phrases, a glowing knob and the percent.

C · Sections

The song split into its parts (between the breaks), like a level map. The part you're in fills and glows; finished parts keep their colour and get a badge: a gold star when every note was Perfect, a tick when clean, the miss count otherwise. Encore phrases as gold marks under them.
